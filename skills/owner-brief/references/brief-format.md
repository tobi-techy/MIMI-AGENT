# Owner brief format (planned)

From the pilot skill (19 Oct 2026). The brief has no tools in this build: until it does,
Mimi says the daily brief is not ready yet. When its tools exist, the brief is:

"Good morning {owner}.
Yesterday: {cash_in_naira} collected (verified). {owed_naira} owed, {overdue_naira} overdue.
Gaps: {rep} {gap_naira}, {n} cartons.
Out of stock: {products}.
Needs you: 1) {action} 2) {action}
Reply with a number for details."

Rules: under 12 lines; lead with money and problems, no padding; at most 3 actions,
biggest naira first; a missing source is one line saying so; never decide for the owner.
