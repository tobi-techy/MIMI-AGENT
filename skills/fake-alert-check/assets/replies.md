# Payment proof wording

From the pilot skill (19 Oct 2026). check_payment_screenshot writes the shop's reply for a
screenshot; send that. For a pasted alert with nothing in the feed yet:

- "Thanks {shop}. I can't see {claimed_amount_naira} in {distributor}'s account yet. Some transfers take a while. I'll confirm once it lands."

Never accuse anyone of fraud; say only that the money has not arrived. Never tell a rep to
release goods until check_payment confirms.
